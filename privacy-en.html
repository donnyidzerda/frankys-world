Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026 · Controller: Donny, KvK 72255269. Contact: info@skep.co.

In short: Franky's World is a calm, ad-free learning app for young children. We show no ads, do no tracking and never sell data. We collect as little as possible. The app works largely offline.

1. What data we process

  • On the device (local): the first name and age you enter for your child, and learning progress (stars, mastered sounds, drawings). Stored in your browser/device local storage.
  • Cloud account & sync: from first use, each child profile gets an anonymous account and the first name + learning progress are synced to our processor Supabase (stored in the EU - Ireland), so progress survives a lost, reinstalled or cleared device. Adding an email + password (or signing in with Google/Apple) is optional and only needed to secure the account and recover it on a new device.
  • Speech recognition: the "listen along" feature uses the device's own speech recognition. Audio and transcripts of your child's voice never leave the device and are not sent to us. Only a pass/fail result is stored locally.
  • Payments (premium): if you subscribe, Creem (our Merchant of Record) processes your payment details and email and remits VAT. We never receive card details - only whether your account is premium.
  • No analytics/ad SDKs, no tracking cookies, no profiling. Only during checkout may our payment partner Creem set necessary (functional) cookies to complete the transaction securely.

2. Why (legal basis)

  • Running the app and saving progress - performance of the contract / legitimate interest in minimal, functional data.
  • Processing payments - performance of the contract.
  • Where consent is required for a child, you as parent/guardian provide it when setting up the app.

3. Children

This app is intended for use by a child under the guidance of a parent/guardian. Purchases and external links are behind a parental gate. We do not collect a child's personal data for ads or profiling. We follow GDPR/GDPR-K principles and the UK Children's Code (high-privacy by default).

4. Processors

  • Supabase - accounts (anonymous, then optional email/SSO) and cloud storage of learning progress, stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Cloudflare - static-asset hosting and the voice-generation + billing proxy (no personal data stored there).
  • ElevenLabs and OpenAI - generate Franky's voice from a fixed set of text phrases (no children's voices, no personal data).
  • Creem (Merchant of Record) - payments, VAT and subscription management.

A data processing agreement (DPA) is/will be in place with each processor. We aim for EU data storage where possible.

5. Retention

Local data stays on your device until you erase it. Synced data is kept while the account is active. You can erase everything anytime via For grown-ups → Privacy & terms → "Delete all my data"; this removes your local and cloud data.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and objection. The fastest erasure route is the in-app button. For other requests: info@skep.co. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in NL: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

7. Changes

We may update this policy. For material changes we update the date above.
