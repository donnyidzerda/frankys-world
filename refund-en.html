Payments for Franky's World are processed by our Merchant of Record, Creem. The policy below applies to subscriptions and one-time purchases.

1. Free trial

Premium subscriptions may include a free trial (7 days). Cancel before the trial ends and you are charged nothing.

2. 14-day cooling-off

As a consumer you generally have a 14-day cooling-off period. For digital services that start immediately with your express consent this right may lapse. If you are not satisfied, contact info@skep.co within 14 days of purchase - we handle reasonable requests generously and in most cases grant a full refund if the service was barely used.

3. Subscriptions

You can cancel anytime via For grown-ups → Premium → Manage subscription (Creem customer portal). On cancellation Premium stays active until the end of the current, already-paid period; already-paid periods are generally not refunded pro rata.

4. One-time (lifetime) purchase

The cooling-off period in point 2 applies to the one-time lifetime purchase. For a valid request within that period we refund the full amount.

5. How to request a refund

Email info@skep.co with your purchase details (the Creem confirmation email). We respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned via Creem to your original payment method.
